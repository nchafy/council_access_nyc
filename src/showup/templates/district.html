Testimony and attendance

Only Council Members can introduce legislation. There is no public petition route onto the agenda.

Speaking at a hearing

  • In person: no pre-registration. Sign-up is at the hearing.
  • Remote testimony, by Zoom web or phone, requires advance registration on the Register to Testify form.
  • Registration for Zoning and Franchises, and for Landmarks, Public Sitings and Dispositions, is on the Land Use page.

Written testimony

  • Accepted up to 72 hours after the hearing is adjourned.
  • DOC, DOCX or PDF, maximum 10 MB, through the same form.
  • Pre-recorded testimony is not played. Audio or video links need a transcript too.
  • Submissions become part of the permanent public record.

Two things nobody publishes

The Council does not publish a registration cut-off time before a hearing, or a per-speaker time limit. We will not guess at either. Chairs usually announce a limit at the start. The hearings office is hearings@council.nyc.gov and 212-482-4219.

After the hearing

The committee may amend the bill, then votes. If it passes by majority it goes to the full Council, then to the Mayor, who has 30 days to sign it, veto it, or do nothing — in which case it becomes law anyway. The Council can override a veto with a two-thirds vote.
