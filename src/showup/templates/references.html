Derived from those datasets

Council district to community board crosswalk
Areal overlap between the council district boundaries (872g-cjhh) and the community district boundaries (5crt-au7u), regenerated when either boundary file changes and committed as crosswalks/council_to_boards.json. The percentages are rounded to a whole number and approximate. On a board page the denominator is that board; on a district page it is that district. The boards dataset also carries a council_district column, which holds 44 distinct values across 59 rows and omits 7 council districts, so it records the board office's district rather than the districts a board covers and is not used for this join.
Written-testimony safe-until timestamp
Hearing start plus 72 hours. Adjournment times are not published and adjournment is at or after the start, so the stamp is a provable lower bound on the real deadline rather than the deadline.
Accommodation request-by date
Three business days before the hearing, against a committed holiday table.

Cited, not fetched

The City Record
{{ m.link(city_record_url, "dg92-zbpx") }} — a few dozen land-use hearing notices a year across all 59 community boards. It is the only City dataset carrying dated board notices, and no City dataset publishes a dated board calendar.
Each board's own website
Agendas, minutes and the meeting location are published there and nowhere in the City's data. A board on its own domain rather than a City one is given as text rather than a link, because the host cannot be verified at build time.

Caveats

  • Community board meeting cadence is quoted exactly as ruf7-3wgc records it. That is the City's record of the cadence, and it has been observed to disagree with what boards publish on their own sites.
  • ruf7-3wgc records the board office address, not the meeting venue. Committees often sit at the office and the full board often does not.
  • Unlike Council committees, which are called by their chair, a community board meets on a standing monthly cadence.
  • Budget requests are an annual filing rather than current activity: the register appears a few times a year, so an entry can be months old. A board page shows the newest edition {{ m.link(register_url, "the register (vn4m-mk4t)") }} published on or before the build date, capped at five requests with the total stated beside it. Priority numbers repeat because each one ranks within a budget category — capital, capital support, expense.
  • Agency replies are quoted rather than sorted into yes and no: the phrasing changes between editions of the same row, and it records a position rather than an outcome. A request is not a commitment and not a funded outcome.
  • The Council Members listed against a board can introduce legislation for that area, and nominate half of the board's members.
  • Council district geometry ships simplified — 132 KB gzipped against 3.8 MB raw, measured to assign the same district as full precision on 99.99% of a citywide lattice.
  • No figure here compares one board or district with another.
